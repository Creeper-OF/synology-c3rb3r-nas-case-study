# Security and research contact

This repository documents a defensive ransomware investigation. The explicitly labeled research Release contains real malware in a password-protected archive; it must not be executed on production systems.

For research questions, corrections, or concerns about misuse of the published specimens, open an issue at https://github.com/Creeper-OF/synology-c3rb3r-nas-case-study/issues . Do not attach credentials, private keys, victim memory, personal logs, or business data. Describe the issue using sample hashes and redacted evidence first.
